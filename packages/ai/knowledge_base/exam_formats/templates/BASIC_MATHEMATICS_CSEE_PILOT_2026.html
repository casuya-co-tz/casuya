SECTION A (60 Marks)

Answer ALL questions in this section. Each question carries 6 marks.

1. (a) Use mathematical tables to evaluate $\frac{\sqrt{0.0456 \times 38.7}}{2.15^2}$ correct to four significant figures.
(b) Three traffic lights show green lights at intervals of 24 seconds, 36 seconds, and 48 seconds respectively. If they light up together at 8:00 AM, at what time will they next show green lights together?
2. (a) Solve for $x$ in the logarithmic equation: $\log_{10}(x^2 + 6) - \log_{10}(x) = 1$.
(b) Rationalize the denominator and simplify the expression: $\frac{3\sqrt{2} + 2}{\sqrt{8} - \sqrt{2}}$.
3. (a) In a class of 40 students, 24 like Mathematics, 18 like Physics, and 5 like neither of the two subjects. Find the number of students who like both Mathematics and Physics using a Venn diagram.
(b) A bag contains 5 red balls and 3 blue balls. Two balls are picked at random one after another without replacement. Calculate the probability that both balls are of the same color.
4. (a) The line passing through points $A(2, -3)$ and $B(6, k)$ is perpendicular to the line $y = -2x + 5$. Find the value of $k$.
(b) Vector $\mathbf{p} = 3\mathbf{i} + 4\mathbf{j}$ and vector $\mathbf{q} = -\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j}$. Find the unit vector in the direction of $2\mathbf{p} - 3\mathbf{q}$.
5. (a) In the geometric figure below, $O$ is the center of the circle, $AC$ is the diameter, and angle $BAC = 32^\circ$. Calculate the magnitude of angle $ADC$.
(b) Given that triangle $ABC$ is similar to triangle $PQR$, where $AB = 6\text{ cm}$, $BC = 9\text{ cm}$, and $PQ = 4\text{ cm}$. Find the perimeter of triangle $PQR$ if $PR = 8\text{ cm}$.

6. (a) Variable $V$ varies directly as the square of $r$ and inversely as $h$. Given that $V = 440$ when $r = 7$ and $h = 10$, find the value of $V$ when $r = 14$ and $h = 5$.
(b) A trader bought an article for TZS 120,000 and marked it to make a profit of 25%. During a clearance sale, she allowed a discount of 10% on the marked price. Calculate the actual percentage profit realized.
7. (a) A school compound is in the shape of a trapezium $ABCD$ where $AB$ is parallel to $DC$. If $AB = 18\text{ m}$, $DC = 12\text{ m}$, and the perpendicular distance between them is $10\text{ m}$, calculate the area of the compound.
(b) Calculate the simple interest earned on TZS 850,000 invested for 3 years at an annual interest rate of 6.5%.
8. (a) Given an Arithmetic Progression (A.P.) whose 3rd term is 11 and 7th term is 27, find the first term and the common difference.
(b) Find the sum of the first 10 terms of the Geometric Progression (G.P.): $3, 6, 12, 24, \dots$
9. (a) A vertical flagpole casts a shadow of length $12\text{ m}$ on horizontal ground when the angle of elevation of the sun is $35^\circ$. Calculate the height of the flagpole to one decimal place.
(b) From the top of a cliff $80\text{ m}$ high, the angle of depression of a boat on the sea is $24^\circ$. Find the distance of the boat from the foot of the cliff.
10. Solve the quadratic equation $2x^2 - 5x + 2 = 0$ by using:
(a) The quadratic formula method.
(b) The method of completing the square.
SECTION B (40 Marks)

Answer ALL questions in this section. Each question carries 10 marks.

11. (a) Find the equation of a straight line that passes through the point of intersection of lines $2x + y = 7$ and $x - y = 2$, and is perpendicular to the line $3x + 4y = 12$.
(b) A small factory manufactures two types of wooden furniture: Chairs ($x$) and Tables ($y$). The constraints for production are:
  • $x + y \le 12$
  • $2x + y \le 16$
  • $x \ge 0, y \ge 0$
If the profit per chair is TZS 15,000 and per table is TZS 25,000, write down the objective function and state the maximum profit.

12. The diagram below represents a right pyramid $VABCD$ on a rectangular base $ABCD$ where $AB = 8\text{ cm}$, $BC = 6\text{ cm}$, and vertical height $VO = 12\text{ cm}$:
(a) Calculate the length of diagonal $AC$.
(b) Calculate the length of the slant edge $VA$.
(c) Find the angle between slant face $VAB$ and the base $ABCD$.
13. (a) Find the inverse of matrix $A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 3 \\ 2 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ and use it to solve the simultaneous equations:
$$\begin{aligned} 4x + 3y &= 10 \\ 2x + 2y &= 6 \end{aligned}$$
(b) A transformation matrix $T = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ maps point $P(4, 2)$ onto point $P'$. Find the coordinates of $P'$ and describe the precise geometric transformation represented by $T$.
14. Function $f(x)$ is defined as $f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 3$ for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.
(a) Express $f(x)$ in vertex form $(x - h)^2 + k$.
(b) State the coordinates of the turning point of $f(x)$ and specify whether it is a minimum or maximum point.
(c) Sketch the graph of $y = f(x)$ showing clearly the axis intercepts.
