## Syllabus overview

**Chapter One: Introduction to Business Studies**
- The concept of a business
  - Business processes (Production, Distribution, Exchange, Consumption)
  - Purpose of a business
- Terminologies used in Business Studies
  - Needs and wants
  - Services
  - Goods (Free and economic goods, Consumer and producer goods, Perishable and durable goods, Merit and demerit goods)
  - Resources
  - Scarcity
  - Opportunity cost
- The concept of Business Studies
  - Importance of studying Business Studies
  - Scope of Business Studies
- Relationship between Business Studies and other subjects

**Chapter Two: Entrepreneurship**
- The concept of entrepreneurship
- Theories of entrepreneurship
- Ways of solving challenges facing entrepreneurship

**Chapter Three: Sole Proprietorship**
- The concept of sole proprietorship
- Formation of a sole proprietorship
- Role of sole proprietorship

**WEEK 1 — NO. 1A (DOUBLE, 80 min):** MC 1.0 | SC 1.1 Explain the concept of
a business | MA "Explain what a business is" | SA "Examine business processes
(production, distribution, exchange, consumption)" | Resources: Business
Studies textbook, local business examples.
- Introduction (10): Asks students to trace where the maize they eat at home
  came from before it reached their plate. / Share ideas tracing the food's
  journey. / Engagement in discussion
- Competence Development (30): Explains in depth the four business processes -
  production, distribution, exchange, and consumption - using the maize
  example and other local cases. / Take detailed notes and draw a flow-chart
  of the four processes. / Correct sequencing of the four processes
- Design (25): Guides groups to trace the four processes for a different local
  product (e.g. a soda, a school uniform). / Trace processes for a chosen
  product in groups. / Correctness of the process flow-chart
- Realization (15): Facilitates group presentations and consolidates key
  points on the board. / Present flow-charts to the class. / Accuracy of
  processes identified
