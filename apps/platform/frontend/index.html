Real people, real days

Made for classrooms like yours

A teacher, students and a father — the everyday people Casuya is built for. If it works for a shared phone in the village, it works for you.

C
Cosmas Dismas
Teacher · Geita

Cosmas marks sixty Form Three papers after school, often by lamplight when the electricity is gone. With Casuya he sets quizzes once and they mark themselves — so he can spend the evening actually helping the students who need him.

B
Bahati Abeld Chusi
Student · Iringa

Bahati shares a phone. He downloads his Form Two Civics notes on the school's good Wi-Fi, then studies on the daladala ride home — no signal, no problem.

N
Nickson Kasmir Tlanka
Student · Karatu

Nickson finds some subjects hard to follow in a crowded classroom. Casuya's interactive lessons let him go back and re-learn at his own pace, again and again, until it clicks.

S
Shedrack Peam Laurent
Student · Arusha

Shedrack wants to track his own improvement, topic by topic, without waiting for end-of-term. Casuya shows him where he's growing every week.

E
Eliya Kikoti
Father · Iringa

Eliya wants to know his child is truly learning, not just "passing." With Casuya he can see real progress — test by test, topic by topic — even on his child's shared phone.

A word from Casuya

"Casuya was built for real people like Cosmas, a teacher; Bahati, Nickson and Shedrack, students; and Eliya, a father — people who share phones, who study through power cuts, and who were always capable of far more than their circumstances allowed.
